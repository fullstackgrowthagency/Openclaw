How it works

Every stage of the trade, automated

A strategy isn't just an entry signal. FSTrading automates every stage between "the market moved" and "the trade is managed correctly."

1

Discovery

Every strategy begins differently. The platform continuously scans for the conditions each one is watching for — no manual refreshing, no jumping between watchlists.

2

Analysis

Finding a candidate is only the first step. Price, volume, volatility, liquidity, technical levels and more are evaluated automatically before anything qualifies.

3

The Waiting

A good setup may not be ready yet. Every candidate is watched at the same time — not the two or three a person can hold in their head at once.

4

Decision & Risk

The order is the last part, not the important part. Risk is sized and decided before a trade exists, not improvised in the moment.

5

Execution

Signal, risk and order come together automatically — entries, stops and targets submitted as one atomic bracket, not separate manual steps.

6

Trade Management

Automation doesn't stop at entry. Positions are actively managed after the fill — the same discipline applied to every trade, every time.

Your account, your custody

Trades through your own broker's accounts

FSTrading isn't a broker. It connects to your own brokerage account's API, using the app key, app secret, and account ID from your broker.

  • Your API credentials are encrypted at rest and never shown back once saved
  • Every order the system places is a real order in your own account — your funds, your custody, your broker
  • FSTrading only automates the decisions
