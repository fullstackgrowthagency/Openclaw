Your own Webull OpenAPI app key/secret -- kept encrypted at rest, never shown back to you in full once saved. This is what runs your trades under your own account's rate limit, not a shared one. Start in sandbox mode.

Live Trading

Off by default. Turning this on routes real orders to a real account with real money -- only available once your connection above is verified. This dashboard's own risk settings (stop-loss, position sizing, daily loss limit, kill switch) still apply exactly the same in live mode as they do in sandbox.

Trading involves risk, especially in live mode. Verify your connection in sandbox first.

Enable Live Trading?

This routes real orders to your real Webull account with real money, using the risk settings currently configured on your dashboard. This is not reversible for any order that has already filled.
