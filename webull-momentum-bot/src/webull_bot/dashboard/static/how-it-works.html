FAQ

Straight answers

Do you take custody of my money?

No. Your capital stays in your own brokerage account. You connect an API key that lets the platform place orders on your behalf, and you can revoke that key at your broker at any time.

What does it cost?

Nothing right now — the platform is in early access. If paid plans arrive later, existing accounts will hear about it before anything changes.

What returns should I expect?

We don't publish performance figures and we won't promise any. Automation makes a strategy consistent; it does not make it profitable. A strategy that loses money by hand will lose money faster automated. Run it in sandbox and judge the results yourself.

Does automation remove the risk?

No. It removes inconsistency in how a defined strategy gets executed. Market risk, strategy risk and software failure all still exist, which is why risk limits and an emergency stop are part of the system rather than an add-on.

Can I stop it mid-trade?

Yes. Close any single position from the dashboard, or use the kill switch to halt new entries and flatten everything currently open.

What do I need to get started?

A brokerage account with API access enabled, and the API key it issues you. Sign up first, then connect the key from your dashboard.
